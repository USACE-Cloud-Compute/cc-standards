# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are building infrastructure used to assess flood risk. Participants in this
project come from Federal service, contractor organizations, academia, and other
agencies, with a range of experience levels and backgrounds. We commit to making
participation open, professional, and free of harassment.

## Expected behavior

- **Assume good faith.** Most disagreements are about a system that is genuinely
  hard, not about a person being careless.
- **Be specific about code, not about people.** "This reduction order is
  nondeterministic" is a review comment. "This is sloppy" is not.
- **Dispute the technical merits, then accept the decision.** Push back with
  reasons; once a maintainer decides, implement it or open an ADR.
- **Give people credit.** This program depends on a small number of contributors.
  Attribution in `AUTHORS`, release notes, and merge comments is not optional.
- **Ask before assuming context.** Much of this codebase predates any given
  contributor. `standards.md` and the ADR log exist so you can find out without
  needing the person who is no longer on the contract.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal or political attacks, publication of others' private
information, uninvited contact outside the project's channels, and any conduct
that could reasonably be considered criminal, discriminatory, or a violation of
USACE policy. This includes conduct in issue threads and pull request review.

Do not bring program-sensitive material into public channels. See
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for reporting, and `standards.md` §4 for what may never
appear in a repository or a fork of one.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces — repositories, issues, pull
requests, discussions, and meetings convened for Cloud Compute — and in external
spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report incidents to `cloudcompute-conduct@usace.army.mil`
<!-- CONFIRM address before publishing -->, or to any named maintainer in
`CODEOWNERS` if you prefer a different route. Reports are reviewed by maintainers
who are not the subject of the report. Where an incident involves a Federal
employee or a contractor's conduct on a contract, it may also be reportable
through USACE or contract channels; we will tell you which, and will not
substitute this process for one that is required.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this code of conduct, and may ban a
participant temporarily or permanently for repeated or severe behavior.

Enforcement decisions are recorded with the Standard Owner, who is not the subject
of the decision. A participant may appeal to the Standard Owner.
